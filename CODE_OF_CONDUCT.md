# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers of `pg_bolt` pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected Behavior

- Focus reviews and discussions on technical merit, safety, correctness, and maintainability.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully and communicate clearly when disagreeing.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time by providing minimal reproducible test cases when reporting issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith conduct in issues, pull requests, or community channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or reject contributions and revoke participation privileges for violations of this Code of Conduct. Reports may be sent confidentially to **opensource@craton.com.ar**. All reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential.

*Adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.*
